Setlist Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Setlist Connect stores the information needed to run gigs and shared setlists. This may include account email, band name, gig details, musician names, musician contact details, songs, setlists, documents, and audio links.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the app, save your work, share gig views with musicians, and support billing for paid plans.

Sharing

When you create or share a gig link, the people with that link may be able to view the gig information included in that shared view. Be thoughtful when adding musician contact information or private documents.

Payments

Payments and subscription management are handled by Stripe. Setlist Connect does not store full credit card numbers.

Service Providers

The app uses service providers such as Supabase for app data and authentication, Stripe for payments, and hosting services for delivery of the web app.
